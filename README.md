# Public Awareness and Behavioral Intentions for Climate Change Mitigation in Vadodara

## Overview

A statistical study of climate change awareness, eco-friendly behaviour,
perceived barriers, emission sources, and environmental trends in Vadodara City.

The project combines primary survey data from 304 respondents with
secondary climate and emission data to investigate behavioural patterns
and environmental trends.

## Objectives

### Primary Objectives

1. Examine the association between education level and climate awareness.
2. Study the relationship between climate awareness and eco-friendly behaviour.
3. Identify major barriers to sustainable lifestyle practices.
4. Identify perceived major sources of carbon emissions.

### Secondary Objectives

1. Analyze temperature trends in Vadodara from 2010–2025.
2. Examine the relationship between population growth and CO₂ emissions.
3. Analyze sector-wise carbon emissions.
4. Forecast sector-wise emissions using ARIMA.

## Methodology

### Sampling

- Sample size: 304 respondents
- Pilot sample: 50
- Sampling method: Stratified random sampling
- Geographic zones: North, South, East and West
- Questionnaire: 17 questions

### Statistical Methods

- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Chi-Square Test of Independence
- Chi-Square Goodness-of-Fit Test
- Goodman-Kruskal Gamma
- Pearson Correlation
- Spearman Rank Correlation
- Pearson Standardized Residuals
- Co-occurrence Analysis
- ARIMA Forecasting

## Key Findings

- Education level was significantly associated with climate awareness.
- Awareness showed a strong positive association with recycling behaviour.
- High cost of eco-friendly products and lack of recycling facilities
  were major reported barriers.
- Industry was the most commonly perceived emission source.
- Maximum temperature showed a statistically significant upward trend.
- Population growth and CO₂ emissions showed a strong positive relationship.
- ARIMA models were used to forecast sector-wise emissions.
